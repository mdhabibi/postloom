# Code of conduct

postloom is a small project, and we want everyone who uses it, reports a problem or sends a
change to feel welcome.

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient, especially with people who are new to software or to English.
- Assume good intent; ask before concluding.
- Criticise ideas and code, never people.
- Keep other people's data private. Don't paste real names, comments or messages from your
  library into issues; describe the problem or use invented examples.

## What isn't acceptable

Harassment, insults, discriminatory remarks, sexual language or imagery, personal attacks,
publishing someone's private information, and any other behaviour a reasonable person would
find unwelcoming.

## What happens

Comments, issues and pull requests that break these rules may be edited, hidden or removed,
and people who keep breaking them may be blocked from the project.

To report a problem privately, use GitHub's private reporting (the repository's
**Security** tab, then **Report a vulnerability**) and say that it is about conduct. Reports
are read by the maintainer only and handled in confidence.

These rules are adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/),
version 2.1.
